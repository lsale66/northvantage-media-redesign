# Brand Brief

## Positioning
- **Business:** Northvantage Media is a real estate media production company — professional photography, cinematic videography, iGuide 3D virtual tours, drone photo/video, and virtual staging for listing marketing and agent branding.
- **Ideal Customer:** Luxury and high-end listing agents in Calgary and surrounding areas (Cochrane, Airdrie, Chestermere, Okotoks) who sell $1M+ properties and need media that matches the price point.
- **Primary CTA:** Reach out directly (call/text/contact) — clients rarely book online without talking to the team first, since Northvantage needs to clarify scope before booking. A "Book Online" link still gets equal visual weight in the hero for agents who already know what they need.
- **Differentiator:** Production quality — cinematic-grade photo and video work that reads as a step above typical listing photography, not just documentation.
- **Positioning Statement:** Northvantage Media is Calgary's listing media partner for agents who sell homes that deserve more than a phone-camera walkthrough. Every shoot is built to make a listing — and the agent's brand — look like it belongs in a higher price bracket.

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Personality:** Confident, refined, craft-driven
- **Tone:** Professional but warm — talks like a skilled collaborator, not a vendor
- **We are:** Premium, cinematic, reliable, Calgary-rooted, production-focused
- **We are NOT:** Corporate, stock-photo-agency generic, loud/glitzy luxury, self-serve/faceless

## Visual Identity

### Color Constraints
None — designer's choice per concept, within a dark navy + orange family (student's explicit direction). Classic navy `#2E4877` from the current site may be woven in for brand continuity but is not a hard requirement.
- **Anchor colors:** None required
- **Avoid:** Light/cream backgrounds, corporate blue, glitzy gold-and-glitter luxury cliches

### Font Constraints
None — designer's choice. Direction: elegant serif display headings paired with a clean sans body, across all concepts (student's explicit preference, informed by wattsupmedia.net reference).
- **Must-use font:** None

> Final palette and fonts are selected per concept direction during brainstorm.

### Visual Style
- **Keywords:** Dark, luxury, cinematic, editorial, restrained
- **Inspiration patterns:** flashvisuals.com (near-black bg, huge condensed sans headline, acid-green script accent word, big stat callouts, high whitespace, luxury-minimalist photography-forward); wattsupmedia.net (orange CTA accent, elegant serif headline, location/rating chip social proof, trust-logo row, video testimonial cards)
- **Do:** Full-bleed real estate photography/video, generous whitespace, restrained single-accent color use, real proof (testimonials, real numbers)
- **Don't:** Generic real-estate-marketing-agency look, stock photography, loud/glitzy luxury cliches (gold foil, glitter), invented/unverifiable stats
