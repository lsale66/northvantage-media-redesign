Northvantage Media — Homepage Concepts

Three redesign directions built from the current site's brand, imagery, and positioning as Calgary's cinematic-grade real estate media partner.

Start the dev server first: astro dev --background, then click a card below (opens on localhost:4321 — check your terminal for the exact port).
Direction 1
Night Showing
Full-bleed twilight exterior photography as the hero, headline in elegant variable-optical serif overlaid directly on the image. Generous whitespace, alternating full-width photo sections and text blocks, thin hairline dividers instead of cards. Large pull-quote testimonials.
Why: Twilight photography is one of the actual services offered, and it's the most cinematic thing Northvantage shoots — leading with it makes the hero itself proof of production quality. Classic navy carries a thread from the current branding so the redesign reads as an elevation, not a rebrand from zero.

Direction 2
Vantage Point
Split-screen hero: bold serif headline and dual CTA on the left, auto-scrolling portfolio grid on the right. Modular card grid for services with rounded corners and pill-shaped CTA buttons; dramatic shadow elevation makes cards feel like physical prints stacked on a light table. Stat callout front and center.
Why: Plays directly on the Northvantage name while giving the grid/portfolio structure a literal reason to exist — the studio-craft direction for agents who want proof of volume and consistency before they call.

Direction 3
Blue Hour
Full-bleed cinematic video loop hero (muted, autoplay) with one massive headline word in extreme size contrast against tiny supporting text. No card shadows; depth comes from soft amber glow blurs instead. Asymmetric section rhythm, portfolio-grid proof with a single-line testimonial marquee.
Why: The boldest, most editorial-studio direction — looks the least like a real-estate-marketing-company and the most like a premium production house, matching the explicit "not corporate/stock-photo" reposition.
